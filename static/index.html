About 4-DOF Wax Carver Studio 2001

4-DOF Wax Carver Studio 2001

A 4-DOF thermal CNC wax carver digital twin: sculpt a face, generate a toolpath, and carve a machinable wax stock in the volumetric SDF viewport.

Pipeline
  1. Choose the bundled Base Head preset or upload a watertight .stl, .obj or .3mf model.
  2. Adjust the facial sculpt sliders and the stock / CAD rig settings.
  3. Press SCULPT & SIMULATE, then scrub or play the transport dock.
